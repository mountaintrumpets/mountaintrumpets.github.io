About Us

Who we are and what we represent

Mountain Trumpets Singing Ministry is a Christian vocal singing ministry from Papua New Guinea. The ministry was established to use the gift of the human voice to worship God, share Christian messages and encourage people.


We do not use musical instruments. Our singing ministry is centered on our voices and our desire to serve God and others through vocal presentations.

Our Ministry

Three important areas of our service

🎤

Evangelism

Sharing Christian messages and the hope of God through vocal singing.

🙏

Worship

Serving churches and gatherings through meaningful vocal presentations that honor God.

🤝

Community

Building fellowship and encouraging young people to use their gifts in service to God.

Our History

Important moments in the journey of Mountain Trumpets

9 May 2025

The Beginning

The group began on a Friday evening. The first members practiced together in Kittinger Pakau's house living room.

10 May 2025

First Song

The first song was presented on Sabbath, marking an important beginning for the group.

June 2025

The Name

The group received the name Mountain Trumpets, reflecting where the members came from.

28 June 2025

Sangurap

The group travelled to Sangurap to sing.

26 July 2025

Lemandi Branch Church

Mountain Trumpets participated in the Sabbath School program.

July 2025

Pelea (Sanga) Branch Church

The group participated in the Sabbath School program.

November 2025

First Uniforms

As the group grew to nine members, Mountain Trumpets received its first uniforms.

31 January 2026

Sangurap Evangelistic Meeting

Mountain Trumpets sang at an evangelistic meeting in Sangurap.
