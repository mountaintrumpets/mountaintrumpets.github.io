About Mountain Trumpets

Mountain Trumpets Singing Ministry is a Christian vocal singing ministry from Papua New Guinea.

The ministry began as a small group of five brothers who shared a desire to sing and serve God. From those humble beginnings, the ministry has continued to participate in Christian programs, worship gatherings, Sabbath School programs and evangelistic meetings.

Our ministry uses the human voice to sing. We do not use musical instruments.

How Mountain Trumpets Came to Be

The story of Mountain Trumpets began on a Friday evening around 9 May 2025.

The five brothers gathered together and practiced and learned a new song in the living room of Kittinger Pakau's house.

This small beginning became the foundation of what would later become Mountain Trumpets Singing Ministry.

The idea of starting the ministry came from Kittinger Pakau.

From Five Brothers to a Ministry

What began with five brothers singing together became a ministry dedicated to using voices to share the Christian message.

Our Journey

9 May 2025 — The Beginning

The singing group began on a Friday evening. The five brothers gathered in the living room and practiced and learned a new song.

10 May 2025 — First Song

The group presented its first song on the following Sabbath.

June 2025 — The Name Mountain Trumpets

The group received the name Mountain Trumpets. The name was chosen because the members came from the mountains.

28 June 2025 — Sangurap

Mountain Trumpets went to Sangurap to sing.

26 July 2025 — Lemandi Branch Church

The group participated in its first Sabbath School program at Lemandi Branch Church.

July 2025 — Pelea (Sanga) Branch Church

The group also participated in a Sabbath School program at Pelea (Sanga) Branch Church.

November 2025 — First Uniforms

Mountain Trumpets received its first uniforms. The history notes record nine members at that time.

31 January 2026 — Evangelistic Meeting

The history notes record Mountain Trumpets singing at an evangelistic meeting at Sangurap.
